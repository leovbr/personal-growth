# Personal Growth

> A 5-year record of becoming better — one day at a time.

## The Mission

This repository is a personal growth system, not a competition.

The first checkpoint is **6 months**. The full horizon is **5 years**.

**Start:** 26 September 2026  
**6-month checkpoint:** 26 March 2027  
**5-year horizon:** 26 September 2031

## Core Rule

> **Small progress, repeated consistently, becomes a different life.**

### Daily minimums

- 📚 Read — maximum 15 minutes
- 🏋️ Train / move
- 💻 Build something useful
- 💰 Track money
- 🧠 Reflect
- 😴 Protect sleep

Missing a day is not failure. Record it honestly and continue.

## Journaling

Each journal entry should answer:

1. What did I do today?
2. What did I learn?
3. What went wrong?
4. What will I improve tomorrow?
5. What am I grateful for?

The goal is not to create beautiful entries. The goal is to leave evidence that the day was lived intentionally.
